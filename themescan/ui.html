Safe-haven check

1 · Download data

Earnings history and analyst actions (the earnings half of conviction). News history is slow because of rate limits.

2 · Live fundamentals

Forward P/E, growth, upside to analyst targets and estimate revisions for every theme (takes a few minutes).

3 · Headlines

Latest news for every currently Leading theme, as a catalyst check.

4 · Backtest

Replays the scanner weekly on history for the selected universe and saves the result for research.

5 · Research

Which signals predicted forward returns, with a train/test split. Needs a backtest result.

6 · Portfolio backtest

Hold the top-N themes by each signal and compare with plain trend / momentum baselines, after costs, with a random-picks test.

7 · Mega-theme clusters

Finds groups of themes that move together and are strong (the "AI trade" shape), how long each has been hot, a lifecycle timeline, and an AI capex fuel gauge. First run scores 5 years of weeks (a few minutes), then it's cached.

8 · News archive & narratives

Download 5 years of monthly headlines per theme (about 45 minutes, resumable; run once), then see which phrases are bursting across themes and how the AI narrative spread.

9 · Demand chains & calendar

Follow spender capex to supplier revenue and margins (AI build-out) and compare with price; list upcoming earnings for theme stocks. Edit chains.yaml to add chains.

10 · Weekly report

One run that logs this week's picks, shows what changed since last week, rotation, news flags, chains, next week's earnings, and saves reports/weekly_DATE.md. Run it weekly (add --ntfy TOPIC on the command line for a phone push).

11 · Forward test

Record today's Strong / Positive / Avoid picks to logs/forward_log.csv, then score them once 21 and 63 trading days have passed. This is the hindsight-free check. Log weekly.

How to read this

Conviction (0–100) = 40% price (rising 200-day trend, relative strength, distance above the 200-day average) + 40% earnings (EPS surprise, beat rate, surprise trend) + 20% news (mean headline tone over the last 30 days, ranked across themes). Price and earnings were blended 50/50 first because earnings was the strongest signal; news was added at 20% because it improved results in 2024 onward and was neutral before, so it is the weakest-evidence component.

Strong ≥ 80 · Positive 65–80 · Neutral 35–65 · Avoid < 35. In tests the average quarterly return vs other themes rose with each tier on both the global and India universes. Themes without usable earnings (e.g. crypto) are price-only (price and news only) and can't be Strong. The News tab shows tone, catalysts, bursting phrases and the AI-narrative spread; news confirmed the AI trade in tests but did not lead it.

Evidence checklist (click a theme): every other source is checked but not scored. Tags show how much to trust each: validated backtested and used in the score; live only valuation, estimate revisions, analyst targets and headlines can't be backtested; tested: no edge macro, analyst upgrades, attention and "not stretched" showed no predictive value, so they are context only.

How to use it: as a tilt. Avoid the Avoid themes and favour Strong / Positive ones across a diversified set. On a broad rule-based universe a concentrated top-5 rotation did not beat random picks; dropping the weakest themes did help (about +1 to +3% a year). Log picks weekly (Data & jobs, card 11) for a hindsight-free check. It describes what is already working; it does not predict the next bull run. This is a research screen, not investment advice.
