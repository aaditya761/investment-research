How to read this

Conviction (0–100) = 50% price score (rising 200-day trend 50%, relative strength 30%, distance above the 200-day average 20%) + 50% earnings (EPS surprise 50%, beat rate 30%, surprise trend 20%). Earnings is the largest single component because it was the strongest signal in the backtests; pure earnings failed on India, so it isn't 100%.

Strong ≥ 80 · Positive 65–80 · Neutral 35–65 · Avoid < 35. In tests the average quarterly return vs other themes rose with each tier on both the global and India universes. Themes without usable earnings (e.g. crypto) are price-only and can't be Strong.

Evidence checklist (click a theme): every other source is checked but not scored. Tags show how much to trust each: validated backtested and used in the score; live only valuation, estimate revisions, analyst targets and headlines can't be backtested; tested: no edge macro, analyst upgrades, attention and "not stretched" showed no predictive value, so they are context only.

Backtests show this describes what is already working and avoids what isn't; it does not predict the next bull run. This is a research screen, not investment advice.

Click a theme row to see its tickers, live headlines and a valuation snapshot. Everything here runs on your machine: the page talks only to 127.0.0.1.
